Privacy Policy

Data processed

During an activity, Walk & Run Tracker processes GPS coordinates, timestamps, elapsed time, distance, pace and the selected activity type. Completed activities are stored locally on the paired phone.

Storage and transmission

Activity and route data is not transmitted to the developer. The app uses no analytics, advertising or cloud-tracking service. Data leaves the app only when the user explicitly exports or shares it. Exported route files may contain precise locations and timestamps.

When a route map is displayed, the companion interface requests the necessary tiles from OpenStreetMap. The request discloses the IP address and approximate displayed area to the OpenStreetMap Foundation, but does not include activity files, account identifiers or an explicitly encoded route.

Permissions

Location permission is required to record route, distance and pace. Without it, activity recording is unavailable. Network access is used to retrieve OpenStreetMap tiles.

Retention and deletion

Activities remain on the paired phone until the user deletes them, clears app data or removes the app. Individual activities can be deleted from the companion interface.

Third parties

OpenStreetMap tiles are provided by the OpenStreetMap Foundation and are subject to its privacy policy. A recipient's own policy applies when the user manually exports or shares an activity.
