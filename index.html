For Even G2

Walk & Run Tracker

Keep every walk or run simple. See elapsed time, distance, pace, GPS status and the current time directly on your glasses.

Simple

Choose walking or running, start in seconds and control the activity from the glasses.

Private

Activities remain locally on the paired phone and are never sent to the developer.

Portable

Choose metric or imperial units, then export FIT, TCX, GPX or JSON files for compatible apps. Exported activities are clearly marked.

Route summary

Review completed activities and their recorded route in the companion interface. Maps use OpenStreetMap tiles with visible attribution.

Italiano

Walk & Run Tracker mantiene semplice ogni camminata o corsa. Sugli occhiali Even G2 mostra tempo, distanza, passo, stato GPS e ora corrente. Le attività rimangono sul telefono associato e possono essere esportate nei formati FIT, TCX, GPX e JSON.
